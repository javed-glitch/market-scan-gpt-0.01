Where heavy volume traded and where price turned, built from free Yahoo hourly data. It shows footprints, not who is trading. Display only: no signals, no orders.
How to read it. Order blocks (Lux-style): after price closes through a swing high or low, the block is the extreme candle of the move that caused it. A block is dropped once price closes through it. Absorption: a bar with at least 2x its normal volume for that hour but a small range, at the lows (accumulation) or highs (distribution). A solid marker means the stock's volume stood at least 1.5x above SPY's in the same hour; a hollow one is market-wide volume, so it is not counted. Volume profile: volume traded at each price over about 20 sessions. Anchored VWAP: average price paid since the window low; price above it means buyers since the low are in profit. The evidence score is a simple tally of those pieces, not a forecast.
