Smart Money - Order Blocks

Cycle dashboard
Goal: spot large amounts of money ploughing in (or out of) a stock, to suggest current and future entry and exit zones over a 1 to 3 week horizon, extendable where confidence in the upside holds. Loose note, to be refined.
Three versions of the same idea, side by side, so we can compare and keep what works. Both read the same data file, so numbers match; only the presentation and the grouping of signals differ.

v1 · Technical

Every signal shown separately
  • Raw order blocks by timeframe (1H, 2H, 4H, daily)
  • Absorption bars, volume profile and anchored VWAP on the chart
  • Evidence score and verdict
Open v1

v2 · Plain

Buy zones and sell zones in everyday words
  • Signals merged into buy and sell zones with a 1–3 dot strength
  • Plain reasons per zone, options and cycle bot cross-check
  • Chart shows only the zones by default; technical detail tucked away
Open v2

v3 · Plain + confidence NEW

v2 plus Low / Medium / High confidence for entry and exit
  • 1 week and 3 to 4 week horizons, on every card
  • Lead vs look-back tags on each reason, plus a "wrong if" price
  • Everything else is v2; new parts are highlighted
Open v3
