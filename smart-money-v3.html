How to read this

  • Buy zone = a price area where lots of buying showed up and the price held or bounced. A limit buy there has support under it.
  • Sell zone = a price area where sellers pushed the price back down. A limit sell or trim there has sellers behind it.
  • Strength = how many separate signals agree on the zone: heavy volume, price turning there before, lots of shares traded there, and options traders' big bets. 3 dots means 3 or more agree.
  • Leading read = signals that look forward: options activity, whether volume turned up when price hit the nearest zone, and strength vs the S&P over 5 days. The lean counts green vs red; results due within 2 days overrides it.
  • Confidence NEW v3 = Low / Medium / High for an entry or exit on a 1 week and a 3 to 4 week horizon. It adds up the signals behind the nearest zone, then applies caps (results due soon, or a zone with one signal). "Wrong if" is the price that breaks the idea.
  • Money flow summarises recent hourly volume: "going in" means heavy buying that held; "going out" means heavy selling at the highs.
This is built from free hourly price and volume data, so it shows footprints, not who is trading. Zones are guides, not guarantees, and prices can go straight through them. Ideas for you to weigh, not advice.
